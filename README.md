# Lab Safety Tour

An interactive, walkable 3D model of a Daytona State College chemistry teaching lab, built in Unity (WebGL) for lab-safety training.

**Play:** https://neogio73.github.io/lab-safety-tour/

Controls: click to look around, WASD or arrow keys to walk, look at safety equipment and press E (or click) to learn about it, F or the on-screen button toggles full screen, Esc frees the mouse. Look at a fume hood sash and click (or press E) to move it: closed, working height, fully open.

Prof. John Picione, Department of Biological and Physical Sciences, Daytona State College.
