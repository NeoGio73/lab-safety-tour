# Lab Safety Tour

An interactive, walkable 3D model of a Daytona State College chemistry teaching lab, built in Unity (WebGL) for lab-safety training.

**Play:** https://neogio73.github.io/lab-safety-tour/

Controls: click to look around, WASD or arrow keys to walk, look at safety equipment and press E (or click) to learn about it, F or the on-screen button toggles full screen, Esc frees the mouse. Look at a fume hood sash and click (or press E) to move it: closed, working height, fully open.

Prof. John Picione, Department of Biological and Physical Sciences, Daytona State College.


**Safety inspection mode:** a short intro explains the task, then each visit hides 5 random hazards (out of 17) around the lab: food or a drink on a bench, a vape, a backpack in the aisle, reagents on top of or the door left open on the flammables cabinet, waste poured on the closed funnel lid, organics down the sink, powder on the balance, broken glass in the trash, a floor spill, a blocked eyewash, shower or extinguisher, a fume hood sash left wide open, a classmate without goggles, or a classmate whose sleeve catches fire (answer the emergency question correctly to put it out). Four students work at the benches and hoods each round. Click each hazard to learn the correct action; whatever you look at gets a gold outline. Press N for a new random inspection.


**Inspection report:** press Enter at any time (or find all 5 hazards) to see a report of what was found and missed, with what to do for each missed hazard, the time, and any wrong answers on emergency questions. Students can screenshot it for credit.
